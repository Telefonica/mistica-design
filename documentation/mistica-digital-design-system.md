## What is Mística?

Mística is Telefónica's Digital Design System. An open and collaborative system for the whole ecosystem of digital products and services in Telefónica.

It is a common design language that provides tools and guidelines with compliant components to deliver your next Telefónica's digital experience.

## Why a Digital Design System?

A digital design system provides consistency, efficiency, and scalability across all digital products and services. It ensures that users have a unified experience while allowing teams to work more efficiently with reusable components and clear guidelines.

## Is Mística for Me?

Mística is for whoever wants to use it.

You can adopt Mística all at once (Big Bang) or do it incrementally (over time). The good thing is that each team can adopt Mística as and when it is needed.

New products are easy since they allow full adoption from the start. A little more effort is required for existing products. If this is your case, we will advise you on how to approach it in a simple manner.

**Contact us via Teams** and we'll tell you how to adopt Mística on your project.

### Which Design Library Do I Need?

#### Native OS

If your digital product is going to be developed in a native OS for iOS or Android, you must use the library corresponding to **Mística Mobile**.

#### Webapp

If you are going to develop a webapp that needs to have mobile and desktop views (with different breakpoints), you will need to make the mobile designs with **Mística Mobile** library and the desktop screens with **Mística Desktop** library.

#### Website

To develop a website with Mística, you will need the **Mística Mobile** library for mobile views and **Mística Desktop** for resolutions higher than 1024px.

## Design Values

Design values are fundamental beliefs that guide our design decisions and ensure cohesion. They define how well different parts work together to help achieve the purpose of the system.

Our mission is to digitize the customer relationship around the services they use, creating meaningful and valuable experiences.

## Design Principles

Design Principles are a set of considerations that form the basis of any good product. They guide teams toward creating consistent, user-centered experiences.

### Localized

Take advantage of localization to make your global products reach specific markets and cultures effectively.

### Flexible

Our design should meet the requirements of users from different countries and contexts, adapting to various needs and preferences.

### Scalable

The design system should grow and adapt with the business needs while maintaining consistency and quality.

### Inclusive

Design for everyone, ensuring accessibility and usability across different abilities, technologies, and contexts.

### Human

Our design should be respectful and revolve around the experience and habits of real people, prioritizing user needs and behaviors.

### Functional

Every design decision should serve a clear purpose and contribute to the overall user experience and business goals.

### Delightful

The design of our product should be an added value to the functional part and should create a positive and valued experience for the user in addition to having its practical benefits.

## Design Languages

Design languages provide the foundation for consistent visual communication across all Telefónica products and services.

## Tokens

Tokens are those system pieces that are more subatomic, such as colors, typography, spacing, and other fundamental design elements. They provide the building blocks for all components and ensure consistency across different platforms and implementations.

### Design Tokens

Design tokens are the core values that define the visual properties of the design system. They include:

- **Colors**: Primary, secondary, and semantic color palettes
- **Typography**: Font families, sizes, weights, and line heights
- **Spacing**: Margin and padding values
- **Border radius**: Corner radius values
- **Shadows**: Elevation and depth effects
- **Animation**: Transition durations and easing functions

## Components

Components are reusable interface elements that combine tokens and design patterns to create consistent user experiences. They range from simple atoms to complex organisms.

### Component Categories

- **Atoms**: Basic building blocks (buttons, inputs, labels)
- **Molecules**: Simple combinations of atoms (search box, navigation item)
- **Organisms**: Complex combinations (headers, forms, cards)
- **Templates**: Page-level structures
- **Pages**: Specific instances of templates

## Layouts

Layouts define the structure and organization of content across different screen sizes and device types. They ensure consistent spacing, alignment, and responsive behavior.

### Layout Principles

- **Grid systems**: Flexible grid structures for content organization
- **Breakpoints**: Responsive design breakpoints for different devices
- **Spacing**: Consistent spacing patterns and rhythm
- **Alignment**: Clear alignment rules and guidelines

## What is Aura?

## Glossary

Definition of the key terms and concepts that appear in Mística documentation.

### Atom

Element from Mística that in combination with other elements (from Mística or custom) solve needs not covered by the system.

### Atomic Constructions

Elements that are built using a combination of atoms or custom elements.

### Design Token

Information associated with a name, at minimum a name/value pair.

In Mística, we use two words to talk about design tokens:

- **Constant**: The value that does not change between brands. Constant is equivalent to "token". In the example below, `color-text-primary` would be the constant.
- **Variable**: The value that changes between brands. In the example below, `#000000` would be the variable. This value can be a hexadecimal color or the name of another variable like `movistarBlue` (that inside stores the hex value).

### Extended Component

These components are built using Mística atoms and styles to meet very specific needs of a team. Due to their characteristics, they are stored in their own separate library (extended library), not in the core library.

### Extended Library

This is a separate library created to store the extended components.

### Primitive

Low-level, highly-flexible components that are meant to be reused for building higher specificity components or atomic constructions.

### Skin

A brand list of values applied to the theme design tokens.

### Slot

A slot is a flexible space enabled in some components for the inclusion of customized content.
